② How peer groups are created & described the missing piece

Three independent ways to decide "which countries are comparable." Each produces a membership map AND a human-readable descriptor, from one shared document (the *_explained source). That same descriptor is what the app shows. Creation is a once-run step; results are frozen and versioned.
